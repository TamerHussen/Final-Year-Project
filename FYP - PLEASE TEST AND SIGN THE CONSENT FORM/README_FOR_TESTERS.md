# The Hunt: Tester Instructions
Project Title: Reinforcement Learning to improve AI behaviour and adaptability in a game environment

Researcher: Tamer Hussen (Birmingham City University)

Thank you for helping me test my Final Year Project! In this game, you will be hunted by two different AI systems. Your feedback will help me determine which one feels more natural and challenging.

## Step 1: Essential Reading
### Before playing, please ensure you have looked at the following files included in this folder:
* Information Letter: Explains what the project is about and what will happen with your data.
* Consent Form: By proceeding to the survey, you are agreeing to the terms outlined in the Information Letter.

## Step 2: How to Test
### To give the best feedback, please follow this order:
* Play against Enemy A: Spend a few minutes trying to hide, distract, and outrun the predator.
* Play against Enemy B: Observe if the predator behaves differently or feels more "robotic" compared to the first.
* Complete the Survey: Use the link below once you have experienced both.

https://forms.office.com/e/Th1shQWB8Q


## Game Controls
* Move - WASD
* Sprint - Left Shift
* Crouch - Left Ctrl
* Jump - Space
* Throw - Left mouse Click
* Pause - Esc

If the mouse feels too fast or slow, I have added a Sensitivity Slider in the settings menu.

## Key Features to Notice
* Sensory System: The predators can see you, hear your footsteps, and even track your scent trail if you stay in one spot too long.
* Stealth: Use the tall grass and bushes to hide. Check your "Exposure Meter" to see how visible you are.
* Distractions: You have a limited number of throwable items. Use them to stun the predator if you are cornered.

## Found a Bug?

If the game crashes or an enemy gets stuck, please let me know in the final text box of the Microsoft Form. This is a prototype, so your bug reports are very helpful!
